# Draft a client reply

## Who this is for

Only a client who already has a folder in `/Users/filipkostkiewicz/projects/jarvis-clients/`. If there is no folder, stop and say Client Success needs to refresh that company first. Do not invent the client from the email thread.

## Two ways to start

New email. Filip says to write a new email to a client and gives the point of the note. Read that client's folder. Offer three subject lines, mark the one used in the draft, and write the message as a new email.

Reply. Filip says to pick up the thread, or a new message has arrived from a client. Search Gmail for the latest thread with that contact and draft a reply to the newest message. Keep that thread's subject. Do not start a new email.

If Filip does not say which, ask before drafting. Read `profile.md`, `onboarding.md`, `decisions.md`, `territory.md`, and `communications.md` either way. If he asks you to check the inbox and nothing new has arrived, say so and do not write a draft.

## Write

One short email. Plain sentences. One purpose. Use the client's first name. Do not restate the whole history. Do not mention ZIP codes, pricing, or lead details that are not in the folder or in Filip's note.

Show the draft in chat with the To address, whether it is a new email or a reply, and the subject. For a new email, list the other subject ideas under the draft. After the sign-off, show this signature so Filip can see the whole note:

```
--
FILIP KOSTKIEWICZ
OWNER | HOLIDAY LIGHTING LEADS
P: 407-376-7175
W: holidaylightingleads.com
```

Gmail inserts that same signature on its own. Leave it out of the Gmail body so it is not doubled.

Then ask Filip with the question tool, with these choices: Approve, Decline, Change the wording. Wait for that answer. A chat reply of approve, decline, or a rewrite means the same thing when the card does not appear.

Approve creates the Gmail message immediately: a new unsent draft, or an unsent reply in the existing thread. Do not ask again whether to save it. Decline stops and sends nothing. A change rewrites the draft and asks again. Do not send.

Do not edit `territory.md`. If the reply would change a ZIP list or a property type, say that in chat for Client Success.
